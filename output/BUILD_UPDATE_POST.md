### Option 2: LinkedIn / Showcase Post

**Transforming Dense Clinical Trials into Handcrafted Interactive Engines.**

Most healthtech communication struggles with a fundamental trade-off: clinical precision vs. public engagement. Academic research stays locked behind medical journals, while social feeds fill up with crash-diet pseudoscience.

For the Steer Health Club Hackathon, I built the **Steer Metabolic Engine** — a dual-engine distribution system designed for Indian metabolic biology:

**1. Tactile Papercraft Explainer Pipeline (Remotion + Headless Chrome)**
Takes clinical paper schemas (like *The Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology* 2021 study on the Indian TOFI phenotype) and programmatically compiles broadcast-grade 9:16 vertical reels:
- Handcrafted paper diorama environments with multi-plane depth physics.
- Real-time coronal DEXA scan viewports showing visceral organ adiposity.
- ElevenLabs neural narration synchronized with ducked procedural marimba scores and Web Audio foley (paper peels, tape strips, chimes).

**2. "The Willpower Defense" — Standalone Playable Arcade**
A zero-friction, zero-signup HTML5 interactive prototype that replaces static BMI calculators:
- Web Audio API real-time sound synthesis (zero external audio dependencies).
- Direct visceral demonstration of biological set-point defense vs. willpower exhaustion.
- 1-click Instagram Story receipt generation for viral peer-to-peer distribution.

**3. Automated Doctor Review Gate**
Every visual asset, video script, and carousel slide is verified against an immutable 13-claim clinical audit ledger linked to verified trial endpoints (NEJM STEP-1, SURPASS-2, Lancet TOFI).

Built with Python, Remotion, TypeScript, Pillow, and FFmpeg.

Core production code: https://github.com/yuvraj-dw/steer-metabolic-engine
